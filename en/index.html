How accuracy works

A useful estimate, with you in control

FitWow combines AI recognition with nutrition references and source checks. The result is designed to make logging faster—not to pretend a camera can measure what it cannot see.

1

Identify the meal

FitWow reads your photo, text, or voice description to identify foods and estimate visible portions.

2

Match the best available basis

Generic foods, barcodes, and clearly identified commercial products follow different source paths instead of being treated as the same kind of item.

3

Review and refine

You can correct foods, portions, and nutrients, or add details the camera missed before saving your log.

👀
What a photo may not reveal

Exact weight, cooking oil, butter, sauces, added sugar, and hidden ingredients can materially change nutrition. Add a short detail or edit the result when you know more.

Nutrition data sources

The source depends on what you log

A banana, a barcode, and a named restaurant meal should not be resolved the same way. FitWow selects a path based on the food type and the information available.

Generic foods

Reference nutrition data

When a suitable match exists, generic foods can use USDA FoodData Central records and FitWow's curated reference layer.

Barcode & catalog

Product databases

Barcode lookup uses FatSecret Platform. Food search can also use Open Food Facts and curated catalog entries.

Named products

Exact-product research

When a brand or restaurant item is clearly identified, FitWow can check manufacturer, restaurant operator, or recognized retailer nutrition information through web search.

No reliable match

AI estimate with guardrails

If an exact source is unavailable, FitWow falls back to an estimate and keeps each item on a consistent basis—per 100 g, serving, or package—to reduce source-mixing errors.

Source availability varies by food and region, and third-party databases can contain incomplete or incorrect entries. For packaged foods, compare the result with the label and adjust when needed.

What makes Meal Effects different

Go beyond calories: learn from how meals feel

Most trackers stop after calories and macros. FitWow lets you connect a meal with what happened afterward, building a personal feedback loop from your own history.

  1. 1
    Record the effect

    Choose a symptom or add your own, then note its severity after a meal.

  2. 2
    Connect it to the meal

    FitWow keeps the effect with that meal so you can review the food, timing, and context together.

  3. 3
    Notice recurring patterns

    When a similar meal appears again, your history can provide a personal reminder—helping you make a more informed choice.
